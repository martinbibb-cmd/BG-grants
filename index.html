About this tool

This is a quick eligibility screener for UK energy support schemes, including:

  • BGET (British Gas Energy Trust)
  • LEAP (Local Energy Advice Partnership)
  • Help4Homes
  • ECO4
  • Great British Insulation Scheme (GBIS)
  • Warm Home Discount, Winter Fuel Payment, and more

Based on your answers it narrows down which schemes you may qualify for and gives you direct links to official providers.

What this tool is not

  • It is not an official eligibility confirmation — final decisions are made by scheme providers.
  • It is not financial or legal advice.
  • It does not submit applications on your behalf.

Always check the official eligibility criteria for each scheme. If you are unsure, consider seeking independent advice.

Data & privacy

This questionnaire asks about benefits, income, and household details only to match eligibility rules.

  • Your answers are not stored on any server or database.
  • No account is required.
  • Answers are processed locally in your browser (in memory) and are lost when you close or refresh the page.
  • We do not set tracking cookies and we do not collect or store your answers.

Postcode lookup (optional): If you tap "Look Up" on the property page, your postcode is sent to postcodes.io and the UK EPC Open Data service to retrieve your property's EPC rating and council tax band. Manual entry is always available as an alternative. We do not store your postcode ourselves.
